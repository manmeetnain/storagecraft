## The five-minute experiment

<Steps>
1. Open the **Universal RAID Planner** and compare RAID 6, RAID 10, and RAID-Z2.
2. Launch the focused RAID-5 Visualizer and fail one disk.
3. Inspect how XOR reconstruction restores the missing blocks.
</Steps>

> StorageCraft is educational engineering software. Validate workload, durability, recovery objectives, and vendor behavior before making production decisions.
