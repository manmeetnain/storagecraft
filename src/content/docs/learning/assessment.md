## Outcome

Demonstrate the full operational workflow and produce evidence that another engineer can review.

**Level:** Assessment · **Time:** 75 minutes · **Prerequisite:** modules 1–8

## Learn

The assessment measures observable work rather than recall. You must preserve service, identify the relevant failure domain, use appropriate commands, avoid unauthorized changes and verify both availability and redundancy.

## Practice

Before assessment, complete one challenge in each Academy technology without hints. Reset the environment between attempts and explain every command before executing it.

## Assess

1. Inspect dual-fabric topology and establish a baseline.
2. Diagnose one FC and one iSCSI incident.
3. Create and activate a safe zoning change.
4. Prove effective state and host-path outcome.
5. Demonstrate checkpoint rollback.
6. Export the Academy report and write a concise incident handoff.

**Pass target:** weighted score of at least 80 with no practical domain below 60.

## Check your understanding

1. Can you explain why every command was necessary?
2. Did you verify redundancy after restoring connectivity?
3. Does your report allow another engineer to reproduce the reasoning?

## Production boundary

The completion record proves performance in this educational subset. It is not a vendor certification and does not authorize production access.
