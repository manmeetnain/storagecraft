# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, disclose uncertainty, and protect confidential information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, publishing private information, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to manmeet.nain@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing StorageCraft.
